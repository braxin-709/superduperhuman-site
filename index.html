SuperDuperHuman is a fast, native Gmail client for Mac, built by Little Pea Productions for our own team. Split inboxes, undo send, keyboard-first everything, and your mail never leaves your Mac.

Built for people who live in their inbox

Everything you need to get through mail quickly, and nothing you don't.

🗂️

Split inboxes

Sort mail into focused streams like people, clients, notifications and newsletters, so the important things rise to the top.

↩️

Undo send

Changed your mind? Pull a message back within a few seconds of hitting send.

⌨️

Keyboard first

Archive, reply, star, snooze and jump between messages without touching the mouse.

👥

Multiple accounts

Add several Gmail accounts, each with its own color and signature.

🔎

Instant search

Search your mail on the device, with Gmail search when you need it.

📇

Contact autocomplete

Addresses fill in from your Google contacts as you type.

✍️

Your Gmail signature

Uses the signature you already set up in Gmail, so messages look the same everywhere.

🍎

Truly native

Written in Swift for macOS. Light on memory, quick to launch, and at home on your Mac.

Private by design

SuperDuperHuman talks directly to Google from your Mac. There's no SuperDuperHuman server in the middle, so your email, contacts and settings stay between you and Google.

  • Signs in with Google's secure OAuth; we never see your password
  • Mail is stored only on your Mac
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling of data
  • Revoke access any time from your Google Account
