Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SuperDuperHuman (“the app”, “we”, “us”), a macOS mail application made by Little Pea Productions, handles your information. The short version: the app runs on your Mac, talks directly to Google, and does not send your data to any server we operate.

1. How the app works

SuperDuperHuman is a native desktop app that runs locally on your Mac. When you sign in, it connects directly from your computer to Google's servers using Google OAuth 2.0. We never see or store your Google password. We do not operate any backend server that receives, processes or stores your email or other Google data.

2. Google data the app accesses

With your permission, the app requests the following Google API scopes:

ScopeWhy the app needs it
https://mail.google.com/To read, display, search, send, draft, label, archive and delete your Gmail messages, as a full email client.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/contacts.readonlyTo suggest recipients from your Google Contacts when you compose a message.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/contacts.other.readonlyTo suggest people you've emailed before (“Other contacts”) when you compose a message.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.settings.basicTo read your Gmail signature so the app can add it to messages you write.
openid, email, profileTo identify which Google account is signed in and show your name, email address and picture in the app.

3. How your data is used and stored

  • Google user data is used only to provide the app's user-facing email features to you.
  • Messages, contacts, settings and account details are stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage on your Mac. Sign-in tokens are kept in the macOS Keychain.
  • Your data is never sent to any server operated by the developer.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not use it to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • No humans at Little Pea Productions read your data.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your data with anyone. Because your data stays on your Mac and is exchanged only between your device and Google, we have nothing to share. The app does not include third-party analytics, advertising or tracking.

5. Limited Use disclosure

SuperDuperHuman's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Your choices and revoking access

  • You can sign out of an account in the app's Settings at any time, which removes that account's local data and tokens from your Mac.
  • You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the app and deleting its data from your Mac removes all locally stored information.

7. Security

Connections to Google use encrypted HTTPS. Sign-in tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain, and local data is protected by your Mac's own account security and disk encryption (such as FileVault).

8. Children

The app is an internal business tool and is not directed to children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
